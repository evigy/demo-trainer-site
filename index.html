Where Details Meet Softness.

Hartwood Equestrian is a boutique training program specializing in thoughtful, personalized development for both horse and rider. We keep our program intentionally small, allowing every partnership to receive clear communication, consistent rides, and an environment that supports long-term progress.

Whether you’re re-entering the ring, starting a young horse, or refining your position, lessons blend classical principles with modern sports psychology to help you feel organized, confident, and connected in the saddle.
