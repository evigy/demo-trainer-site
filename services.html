Services & Rates

Structured Support, Season After Season.

Lesson programs, training rides, and tailored offerings to keep horses soft, riders organized, and progress steady.

Lesson Programs

All lessons include time for questions, goal-setting, and a simple homework plan so you leave knowing exactly what to practice.

Private Lessons

One horse, one rider, full focus. Ideal for riders working through specific challenges, green horses, or those who prefer a quieter learning environment.

  • 60 minutes mounted + discussion
  • On your horse or selected school horse
  • Video feedback available on request

$95 per lesson · Packages from $360 / 4 rides

Small Group Lessons

Low-pressure group sessions limited to four riders, focusing on track craft, ring awareness, and coursework in a supportive setting.

  • 75 minutes including warm-up patterns
  • Riders grouped by experience level
  • Great for friends or barn groups

$70 per rider · 3–4 riders

Guided Trail Sessions

Calm, structured hacks around the property and adjoining trails, building confidence outside the arena for both horse and rider.

  • 60–75 minutes, walk/trot with optional canter
  • Safety-focused with groundwork as needed
  • Perfect for young or anxious horses

$85 per session

Training & Select Services

For riders seeking deeper support, Hartwood offers ongoing training options customized to your schedule, goals, and horse’s needs.

Partial Training

A blend of professional schooling rides and lessons to keep your horse tuned while you stay actively involved in the program.

  • 2 training rides + 1 lesson per week
  • Customized ride plan updated monthly
  • Limited availability

$820 / month

Full Training

Comprehensive care for horses in development, rehab, or show preparation, with consistent schooling and detailed communication.

  • 5 sessions per week (rides or lessons)
  • Weekly progress summaries
  • Show coaching available separately

$1,450 / month

Rider Strategy Sessions

Unmounted sessions focusing on show planning, confidence tools, and ride-review using video and tailored exercises.

  • 45 minutes via in-person or video call
  • Ideal before show season or new goals

$65 per session
